Deepak Mahajan (mob. 9812345678), employed as purchase officer, raised
duplicate invoices in favour of Orbit Traders, a firm with no premises at its
registered address. Payments totalling Rs 14,80,000 were released to

The complainant further states that accused Deepak Mahajan is related to one
Sunita Mahajan (mob. 9876120044), who is the sole proprietor of Orbit Traders

made on 18/07/2026 against invoice OT/2026/318, for goods which the complainant
states were never received at the factory.

A vehicle bearing registration PB 10 CD 5566 was used to collect signed
delivery challans from the Focal Point premises on 18/07/2026 and 02/08/2026.

The complainant requests registration of a case and investigation into the
account and the firm.
