## What the official SIH guidelines actually say — checked 2026-09-07

Source: **`SIH2026-Guidelines-College-SPOC.pdf`** on sih.gov.in — authored by the
Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell, PDF created **2026-06-02**, 26 pages.

**SIH asks a team for exactly one thing: the idea, submitted on the portal by the
team leader.** There is no national requirement for a progress report, for images,
or for a final PPT. So **no official link for those two will ever exist** — they
are PEC-internal and only Dr. Goel can say where they go.

**And her five deliverables are explained by what *she* has to file.** The
guidelines put an obligation on the SPOC, not on teams (p. 9):

> SPOCs must upload a detailed **Internal Hackathon report (maximum 15 pages)** on
> the dashboard portal — event overview (1 p), **event photos (2 p)**,
> **participating teams' pictures (2 p)**, judging process (1 p), jury panel (1 p),
> nominated top teams (1 p), judges' information (1 p), totals (2 p), news articles
> (1 p), social media promotion (1 p) with #sih2026 and #smartindiahackathon2026.

She also has to supply an **Internal Hackathon Image/Video Link** and participant
counts (p. 8). That is why the images carry the only stated penalty: **four pages
of her mandatory report are photographs**, and she cannot file without them.

**Registration path (p. 6, p. 12, p. 16):** only teams selected in the internal
hackathon may be registered; the SPOC nominates; **the team leader gets portal
login credentials only after the SPOC uploads the team.** Nothing about the
national round is in Kanwar's hands until she acts.
